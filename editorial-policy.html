Editorial Policy

The standards behind our educational business intelligence.

Mission

Valverde Intelligence translates business strategy, administration, operations, technology, leadership, and continuity into clear, practical education for organizational decision-makers.

Independence and commercial context

This publication is owned by Valverde Group Inc. and may discuss subjects related to its consulting capabilities. Calls to action and commercial relationships should be clearly identifiable. Educational coverage will not be presented as independent third-party endorsement.

Source standards

Legal, regulatory, consumer-protection, statistical, and time-sensitive claims should be verified against current primary or authoritative sources. Sources should be linked near the relevant content when useful. Opinion, inference, and general operational frameworks should be distinguishable from sourced facts.

Corrections and updates

Material errors should be corrected promptly. Substantive updates should include a reviewed or updated date when practical. Older articles may remain available for historical context but should not be represented as current when circumstances have changed.

AI-assisted production

AI tools may assist research organization, drafting, editing, imagery, transcription, or production. Valverde Group remains responsible for review and publication. AI output is not treated as an authoritative source, and sensitive client information should not be entered into unapproved systems.

Testimonials, affiliations, and conflicts

Material relationships, employee testimonials, sponsorships, affiliate compensation, or other conflicts should be disclosed clearly and close to the relevant claim. Reviews must not be fabricated or materially altered.

Responsible claims

Content must not promise business, credit, financing, tax, legal, investment, or financial outcomes. Hypothetical examples should be labeled. Past performance and illustrative results are not guarantees.

Credit content boundary

Credit-related content is limited to general consumer education, business administration, financial readiness, official rights resources, and risk awareness. Valverde Group Inc. is not a credit repair organization and does not sell disputes, deletions, score changes, or financing approvals.

Professional review

Content that could materially affect legal rights, taxes, regulated activity, investments, lending, estates, healthcare, employment, privacy, or security should direct readers to qualified professionals and should receive subject-matter or legal review where appropriate.

Editorial promise

Clear scope, responsible sourcing, visible commercial context, and corrections when needed.
