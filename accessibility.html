Accessibility Statement

Valverde Group is committed to making this publication usable by as many people as reasonably possible.

Our approach

We design toward recognized accessibility practices, including meaningful heading structure, keyboard navigation, readable contrast, responsive layouts, visible focus behavior, descriptive link text, text alternatives for meaningful media, and captions or transcripts for future video content.

Current features

  • A skip link for keyboard and assistive-technology users
  • Semantic headings, navigation, articles, and landmarks
  • Layouts that adapt to smaller screens and browser zoom
  • Text labels in addition to color and visual styling
  • No automatically playing audio
  • HTTPS delivery through GitHub Pages

Future media

When video is added, we intend to provide captions and, where appropriate, transcripts or descriptive alternatives. Motion effects should respect device or browser accessibility preferences as the publication evolves.

Limitations

Accessibility is an ongoing process, and automated testing alone cannot identify every barrier. Third-party destinations and embedded services may not be controlled by Valverde Group.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, use the verified contact information on the official Valverde Group corporate website and describe the page, feature, assistive technology, and problem. We will review reasonable reports and work toward an appropriate solution.
