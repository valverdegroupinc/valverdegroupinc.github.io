Privacy Notice

How this publication handles information in its current static-site form.

Current configuration: Valverde Intelligence does not currently provide account registration, comments, payment, newsletter signup, or a contact form. This notice must be updated before those features or analytics are added.

Information you provide

This static publication does not currently ask visitors to submit personal information directly. If you follow a link to the Valverde Group corporate website or a third party, that destination’s privacy practices apply.

Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with requests to deliver and secure the service, such as IP address, browser or device information, timestamps, and requested pages, under GitHub’s applicable notices and terms.

External resources

The site currently requests web-font files from Google Fonts. A visitor’s browser may connect to Google to retrieve those resources, allowing Google to receive technical request information. Links to government sources and other websites may also involve separate third-party data practices.

Cookies and analytics

Valverde Group has not intentionally installed advertising pixels, behavioral analytics, or marketing cookies on this GitHub publication. Hosting or embedded third-party services may use technical mechanisms outside our direct control. We will update this notice and implement appropriate consent controls before adding optional analytics or advertising technologies where required.

Information minimization

Future features should collect only information reasonably necessary for a disclosed purpose, limit access, protect the information, and establish retention and deletion practices appropriate to its sensitivity.

Sensitive information

Do not submit Social Security numbers, bank credentials, payment-card data, passwords, private keys, health information, consumer reports, or other sensitive material through any informal channel or future site feature unless expressly requested through an approved secure process.

Children

This business publication is directed to adults and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You may browse without creating an account. You can control browser features and leave the site at any time. Rights regarding personal information vary by jurisdiction and the information involved.

Security

No online service can guarantee absolute security. We use a static, HTTPS-hosted architecture that limits direct data collection, and we will reassess safeguards as features change.

Updates and contact

Material changes will be reflected through a revised effective date. For a privacy question, use the verified contact information on the official Valverde Group corporate website. A dedicated privacy contact and applicable state-specific disclosures should be added after legal review.

Data principle

Collect less. Restrict access. Keep only as long as necessary. Protect according to risk.
